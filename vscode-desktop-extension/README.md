## Develop for SiMa DevKits in VS Code

Keep your editor, terminal, Git and the extensions you already use, including your AI agent. SiMa Neat Studio adds:

<img src="media/marketplace/readme-apps.png" width="16" height="16" align="absmiddle" alt="">&nbsp;&nbsp;**Apps** — run prebuilt examples on your DevKit in one click, model and sample video included.

<img src="media/marketplace/readme-models.png" width="16" height="16" align="absmiddle" alt="">&nbsp;&nbsp;**Model Registry** — browse and add models with your SiMa account.

<img src="media/marketplace/readme-insight.png" width="16" height="16" align="absmiddle" alt="">&nbsp;&nbsp;**Insight** — video viewer, stats and device health beside your code.
